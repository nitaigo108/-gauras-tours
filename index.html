The Journey

A thoughtful rhythm of pilgrimage, practice, exploration, community and reflection.

☀️ Daily Morning Yoga
Begin the day grounded, open and present.
🌙 Evening Reflection
Kirtan, spiritual readings and time together.
NOV 8

Arrival in Delhi

Arrive in Delhi, transfer to the hotel, settle in and prepare for the journey ahead.

NOV 9

Journey to Rishikesh

After breakfast, travel north toward the Himalayan foothills and the sacred city of Rishikesh.

NOV 10

Waterfall Hike & Ganga Puja

Morning yoga, a beautiful waterfall hike and an evening experience beside the sacred Ganga.

NOV 11

Devi Temple Sunrise

Rise early for a sunrise visit to Devi Temple followed by a scenic hike down through the Himalayan landscape.

NOV 12

Vashishtha's Cave

Visit the ancient meditation cave associated with the sage Vashishtha and spend contemplative time beside the Ganga.

NOV 13

Haridwar Excursion

Explore one of India's most sacred pilgrimage cities and experience its powerful river and temple atmosphere.

NOV 14

Neelkanth Mahadev Temple

Journey into the hills for a sacred temple visit surrounded by the natural beauty of the Himalayan region.

NOV 15

Journey to Vrindavan

Travel from Rishikesh to Vrindavan and enter the devotional heart of Braj.

NOV 16

Govardhan Parikrama & Seva

Experience a portion of sacred Govardhan parikrama followed by meaningful seva at the widows' ashram near Radha-kunda.

NOV 17

Krishna-Balaram & Radha-Raman

Attend the morning spiritual program, visit the historic Radha-Raman Temple and spend the evening with sacred readings and spiritual discussion.

NOV 18

Barsana

Travel into sacred Braj to visit Barsana, meet saints and sadhus and experience places rich with devotional history.

NOV 19

Return to Delhi

Travel to Delhi for onward flights, with an optional Taj Mahal sunrise experience depending on travel schedules.
